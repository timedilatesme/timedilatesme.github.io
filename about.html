About

Hi, I'm Paras.

I'm a PhD student in Physics & Astronomy at Stony Brook University, working with Prof. Simon Birrer on strong gravitational lensing: using it to constrain dark energy and to study quasar accretion disks, and building the simulations behind both. I'm a member of the LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration (DESC) and the LSST Strong Lensing Science Collaboration (SLSC), and one of the lead developers of SLSim.

Before lensing, at Stony Brook, I worked with Prof. Jin Koda on the dynamics of barred spiral galaxies, and how the pattern speed of a bar varies with radius.

I studied Engineering Physics at IIT Delhi, where I worked with Prof. Suprit Singh on magnetic fields around distant galaxies, measured with background quasars. With him I later took part in polarimetry of the solar corona during the total solar eclipse of 8 April 2024. In the summer of 2022 I studied the neutral interstellar medium with Prof. Peter G. Martin and Dr. Antoine Marchal at the Canadian Institute for Theoretical Astrophysics, as a Mitacs Globalink Research Intern.
