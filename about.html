About

Hi, I'm Paras.

My way into astronomy ran through physics. I studied Engineering Physics at IIT Delhi, and in 2022 began working with Prof. Suprit Singh on magnetic fields around distant galaxies, measured with the polarized radio light of quasars behind them. That summer I was a Mitacs Globalink Research Intern at the Canadian Institute for Theoretical Astrophysics in Toronto, studying the neutral interstellar medium with Prof. Peter G. Martin and Dr. Antoine Marchal.

With Prof. Singh's group I also chased two total solar eclipses: a rehearsal in Exmouth, Australia, in 2023, and four minutes of totality in Dallas, Texas, in 2024, measuring how the light of the corona is polarized.

At Stony Brook I first worked with Prof. Jin Koda on the dynamics of barred spiral galaxies, and how the pattern speed of a bar varies with radius. Since 2025 I have worked with Prof. Simon Birrer on strong gravitational lensing, the work described on the research pages.
